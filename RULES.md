# Clues by Sam Rules

This file is an edited reference summary of the public instructions at [Clues by Sam](https://cluesbysam.com/).

Clues by Sam owns the game and its original instructions. This summary supports the keyboard-extension project.

## Tips

- The names are in alphabetical order.
- Select the top corner of a card to add a color tag.
- Use color tags to record deductions or hypothetical choices.
- Select a revealed clue to dim or restore it.
- Use dimmed clues to identify clues that are not currently relevant.
- The game highlights referenced names and professions.
- Select a clue rapidly to make its references flash.
- For more information about clue terms, select **Inspect** and then select a revealed card.

## Basic Rules

- Each person is either innocent or criminal.
- A profession does not determine innocence or guilt unless a clue states this relationship.
- Each person tells the truth, including each criminal.
- The puzzle always has a logical next choice. A guess is not necessary.
- The game prevents choices that do not have sufficient logical support.

## Grid Terms

- The grid has five horizontal rows. The row numbers are 1 through 5.
- The grid has four vertical columns. The column letters are A through D.
- A **neighbor** is in an adjacent horizontal, vertical, or diagonal position.
- One person can have up to eight neighbors.
- **Above** and **below** refer to positions in the same column.
- **Left** and **right** refer to positions in the same row, from the player's view.
- **Directly** left, right, above, or below means the adjacent position in that direction.
- **Between** refers only to the people between two specified people. It excludes the two specified people.
- **Connected** means connected through horizontal or vertical adjacency.
- A connected group cannot have a different type of person between its members.
- A group of one person is connected.
- A **corner** is one of the four corner positions.
- An **edge** contains one of the 14 positions around the outside of the grid. The edge includes the corners.

## Groups and Counts

- **All** means that at least one matching person exists. It does not require more than one.
- **One of the innocents** or **one of the criminals** means that the specified group contains at least two people.
- **One of several** or **one of multiple** also means that the specified group contains at least two people.
- These uses of **one of** differ from the rule for **all**.
- **Common neighbors** are people who neighbor both specified people. The two specified people are not their own common neighbors.
- **Share** means **have in common**.
- For example, two people who share three innocent neighbors have three common innocent neighbors.
- **In total** means the sum of all specified groups.
- For example, two criminal cops and coders in total can mean one cop and one coder.
- The same total can also mean zero cops and two coders, or two cops and zero coders.
- **The most** always means uniquely the most. No other person or group can have the same count.
- An even number is divisible by two. Examples are 0, 2, 4, and 6.
- An odd number is not divisible by two. Examples are 1, 3, 5, and 7.
- **More** does not require the smaller group to contain a person.
- For example, a row with two innocents has more innocents than a row with zero innocents.
- A stated number is exact unless the clue states otherwise.
- For example, **two criminal coders** means that exactly two coders are criminal.

## People and Professions

- **Person** means any character in the grid.
- A person can be a human, an animal, or another nonhuman character in a special puzzle.
- A cop is not more likely to be innocent than an accountant unless a clue states this relationship.

## Conditional Logic

The statement **if A, then B** has these meanings:

- If A is true, B is true.
- If B is false, A is false. This equivalent statement is the **contrapositive**.
- If A is false, the statement gives no conclusion about B.
- If B is true, the statement gives no conclusion about A.

The statement does not mean **A if and only if B**.
